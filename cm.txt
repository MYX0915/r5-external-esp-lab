补充参考：两个仓库的 Glow 写入序列完整对照

xTeJk/External-Apex-Tool (main) main.cpp:
  Entity+GLOW_CONTEXT(0x3C8) = 1
  Entity+GLOW_VISIBLE_TYPE(0x3D0) = 2
  Entity+GLOW_TYPE(0x2C4) = GlowMode{101,101,100,100}
      GlowMode = 4 x int8: GeneralGlowMode/BorderGlowMode/BorderSize/TransparentLevel
  Entity+GLOW_COLOR(0x1D0/1D4/1D8) = RGB

memcpyy/R5Apex-UserMode (master) Players.hpp:
  Entity+0x480 = true
  Entity+0x2C4 = 1
  Entity+0x1D0/1D4/1D8 = RGB
  Entity+0x2B0..0x2C8 与 0x2DC = FLT_MAX

对照:
  颜色 0x1D0/1D4/1D8 —— 两份完全一致（最可信）
  GlowMode 0x2C4    —— 两份一致
  启用写法不同: 0x480 bool vs 0x3D0=2
  上下文 0x3C8=1    —— 仅 xTeJk
